Professional Patio Cleaning You Can Trust

Over time, patios naturally collect dirt, moss, algae, weeds, and staining from weather and everyday use. Unfortunately, these surfaces can quickly become slippery, dull, and unsafe if left untreated.

At Pacys, we use professional pressure washing equipment and safe cleaning methods to deep clean patios without causing unnecessary damage. We take pride in every job and always aim to leave your outdoor space looking fresh, clean, and refreshed.

No rushed jobs. No harsh sales tactics. Just honest, reliable service with quality results.

Our Patio Cleaning Services Include

Pressure Washing

We remove built-up dirt, grime, moss, algae, and surface staining using professional-grade pressure washing equipment.

Weed & Moss Removal

We clear unwanted weeds and moss growth from joints and patio surfaces to improve appearance and reduce regrowth.

Black Spot Treatment

We treat stubborn black lichen spots and deep staining that standard cleaning often cannot remove.

Re-Sanding (Block Paving)

Where needed, we can re-sand joints after cleaning to help stabilise block paving patios.

Patio Sealing

Optional sealing services are available to help protect your patio from future staining, moss growth, and weather damage.
