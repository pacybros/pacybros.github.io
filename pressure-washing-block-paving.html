The Full Block Paving Cleaning Process

Every block paving cleaning project starts with a full assessment of the condition of the surface. This allows us to identify heavy staining, sunken areas, damaged blocks, weed growth and any areas requiring additional care during the cleaning process. We also check drainage and surrounding areas before starting any pressure washing work.

The first stage of the process is weed removal and surface preparation. We remove loose debris, pull larger weeds and apply professional treatment where required. Removing weeds before pressure washing helps achieve a cleaner finish and reduces the chance of regrowth returning quickly after the clean.

Once the surface is prepared, we begin pressure washing the block paving using commercial rotary surface cleaners and lance systems. These machines clean deep into the textured surface of the blocks and joints to remove dirt, algae, moss, black spots and grime. The cleaning process restores the natural appearance of the paving and brightens the overall area significantly.

Special attention is given to heavily stained areas including tyre marks, oil spots and dark organic growth. In some cases, specialist cleaning solutions may be used alongside pressure washing to improve results without damaging the paving surface.

Why Resanding Block Paving Is Important

One of the most important parts of pressure washing block paving is resanding the joints after cleaning. During pressure washing, the original jointing sand between the blocks is naturally removed. This is completely normal and expected during deep cleaning.

Without replacing the jointing sand, the blocks may become loose over time, which can lead to movement, sinking and instability. Fresh kiln dried sand is brushed carefully back into all joints once the surface is completely dry. This helps lock the paving blocks firmly together and restores structural support across the entire surface.

Resanding also helps reduce weed growth by filling the joints properly and limiting spaces where weeds can take root. Properly sanded block paving not only looks cleaner and sharper, but also performs better long-term.

At Pacys, we always recommend allowing the paving to dry fully before resanding to ensure the kiln dried sand settles correctly into the joints. This creates a much more durable and professional finish compared to rushing the process.

Block Paving Sealing Services

After pressure washing and resanding, many customers choose to have their block paving sealed. Sealing is an optional but highly beneficial step that helps protect the surface against future staining, weed growth and weather damage.

Professional block paving sealer penetrates the surface and joints to create a protective barrier. This barrier helps reduce water penetration, slows down algae and moss growth, enhances colour and makes future maintenance easier. Sealed block paving often stays cleaner for longer compared to unsealed surfaces.

There are different types of sealers available depending on the desired finish. Some customers prefer a natural matt finish while others choose a wet look sealer to enhance the colour and appearance of the paving. We can advise on the best sealing option depending on the condition and style of the paving.

Before sealing, it is important that the paving is fully dry and freshly resanded. Applying sealer too early can trap moisture and affect the final finish. We ensure all preparation stages are completed correctly before any sealer is applied.
